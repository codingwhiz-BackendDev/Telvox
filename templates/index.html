FAQ

Questions answered

What is Telvox and how does it work?
Telvox is a virtual phone number service powered by Twilio's VoIP network. You get a real phone number from US, UK, Canada, Australia and more — without a physical SIM. Use it to make and receive calls and SMS via our web or mobile app from anywhere in the world.
Do I need a SIM card or special hardware?
No SIM card, no hardware, no browser extension required. All you need is an internet connection. Use our browser-based web dialer on desktop or download the iOS/Android app for mobile.
Can I use Telvox numbers for app verifications?
Yes — Telvox numbers work for SMS verifications on most major platforms including WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, and Twitter/X. These are real numbers provisioned through Twilio, not recycled disposable numbers.
Can I use Telvox from anywhere in the world?
Absolutely. Whether you're in Nigeria, India, Brazil, Japan, or anywhere else — as long as you have an internet connection, you can make and receive calls using your virtual number. That's the whole point.
What happens if I run out of credits?
Incoming calls and SMS are always free. Credits are only used for outgoing calls and SMS. If your balance hits zero, top up instantly from the billing dashboard. Your number stays active as long as your subscription is paid.
Is there a cancellation fee?
None whatsoever. Cancel anytime from your dashboard — no forms, no calls, no fees. Your number remains active until the end of your billing period.
